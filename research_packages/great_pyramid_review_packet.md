# Great Pyramid evidence review package

**Episode:** The Great Pyramid Mystery: The Oldest Cover-Up In History  
**Package status:** REVIEW REQUIRED  
**No source or claim in this package is approved for narration.**

## Editorial guardrail

The phrase **“oldest cover-up” is not supported** by the source set. The defensible story is stronger when it separates what modern measurement has detected, what contemporary records document, what archaeology has uncovered, and what remains unknown. Do not imply deliberate concealment, a conspiracy, or a secret chamber.

## Source review queue

### gp_src_001 — ScanPyramids Big Void

- **Paper:** Morishima et al., “Discovery of a big void in Khufu’s Pyramid by observation of cosmic-ray muons,” *Nature* 552, 386–390 (2017).
- **Original record:** https://www.nature.com/articles/nature24647
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1038/nature24647
- **Review anchor:** Abstract, pp. 386–390, figures, Extended Data Table 1.
- **Candidate relevance:** Reports a large void above the Grand Gallery with a minimum length of 30 metres. The study reports detection using three muon technologies and independent analyses.
- **Critical limitation:** The paper explicitly says the intended purpose of the void is unknown. Call it a *void*, not a room, tomb, treasure chamber, or cover-up.
- **Status:** UNREVIEWED

### gp_src_002 — North Face Corridor

- **Paper:** Procureur et al., “Precise characterization of a corridor-shaped structure in Khufu’s Pyramid by observation of cosmic-ray muons,” *Nature Communications* 14, 1144 (2023).
- **Original record:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-36351-0
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-36351-0
- **Review anchor:** Abstract, Results, Discussion, Table 1.
- **Candidate relevance:** Characterizes a corridor behind the North Face Chevron. Independent Nagoya and CEA estimates place it at roughly 9 metres long with a cross-section around 2 metres by 2 metres.
- **Critical limitation:** This North Face Corridor is distinct from the 2017 Big Void. The paper does not establish the corridor's original function.
- **Status:** UNREVIEWED

### gp_src_003 — Merer logbook / Wadi el-Jarf papyri

- **Edition:** Pierre Tallet, *Les papyrus de la mer Rouge I. Le journal de Merer*, IFAO, MIFAO 136 (2017), ISBN 9782724707069.
- **Publisher catalogue:** https://www.ifao.egnet.net/publications/catalogue/9782724707069/
- **Review anchor:** IFAO catalogue entry and its linked English/Arabic appendices and translations. Inspect the actual papyrus transcription and translation.
- **Candidate relevance:** IFAO describes the logbooks as records of Inspector Merer's team transporting limestone from the Tura quarries to Khufu's Great Pyramid while it was under construction.
- **Critical limitation:** A publisher's catalogue summary is not a substitute for checking the underlying logbook. Record the exact papyrus, entry, and page.
- **Status:** UNREVIEWED

### gp_src_004 — Heit el-Ghurab / Lost City of the Pyramids

- **Project record:** Ancient Egypt Research Associates (AERA).
- **Original record:** https://aeraweb.org/projects/lost-city/
- **Review anchor:** Project-page sections on chronology, settlement extent, excavation location, and site functions.
- **Candidate relevance:** AERA reports a 4th Dynasty settlement south of the Sphinx, over 7 hectares in extent, with workshops, bakeries, warehouses, and administrative spaces. AERA says the settlement served the pyramid complexes of Menkaure and Khafre and *probably* Khufu.
- **Critical limitation:** Preserve the qualification “probably Khufu.” Do not say every resident directly built Khufu's pyramid.
- **Status:** UNREVIEWED

### gp_src_005 — Graffiti on Khufu's second boat-pit cover stones

- **Paper:** Kazumitsu Takahashi and Akiko Nishisaka, “Some Notes on the Graffiti Written on the Cover Stones from the Second Boat Pit of Khufu,” *Bulletin of the Society for Near Eastern Studies in Japan* 59(1), 2–13 (2016).
- **Original record:** https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jorient/59/1/59_2/_article/-char/en
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5356/jorient.59.1_2
- **Review anchor:** Abstract and full PDF, pp. 2–13; inspect photographs and the authors' interpretation.
- **Candidate relevance:** Examines ink graffiti on cover stones from Khufu's second boat pit and interprets the markings as evidence of different stages and organizations involved in stone transport.
- **Critical limitation:** The abstract uses qualified language such as “seem” and “presumed.” Keep this as the authors' interpretation, not an unquestioned fact.
- **Status:** UNREVIEWED

### gp_src_006 — Great Pyramid Scanning Project / bedrock features

- **Report:** AERA, *Annual Report 2023–2024*, “The Great Pyramid Scanning Project.”
- **Original record:** https://aeraweb.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/AERA_2024.pdf
- **Review anchor:** Printed report page 7.
- **Candidate relevance:** Describes the ongoing recording of bedrock features around Khufu's pyramid, including postholes, lever sockets, quarry channels, and builders' marks.
- **Critical limitation:** This is an annual fieldwork report about an ongoing survey, not a final reconstruction of the complete building method.
- **Status:** UNREVIEWED

### gp_src_007 — Official monument baseline

- **Record:** Egyptian Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities, “The Great Pyramid.”
- **Original record:** https://egymonuments.gov.eg/monuments/the-great-pyramid/
- **Review anchor:** Main monument description.
- **Candidate relevance:** Official baseline context for Khufu, the pyramid's reported original height, internal chambers, casing stones, boats, and estimated construction duration.
- **Critical limitation:** The 10–20 year construction duration is an estimate. The page itself says the precise method is not entirely certain.
- **Status:** UNREVIEWED

## Claims this package does not support

- “The Great Pyramid was part of an ancient cover-up.”
- “The Big Void is a hidden chamber, treasure room, or secret tomb.”
- “Archaeologists know exactly how the Great Pyramid was built.”
- Claims of aliens, lost advanced technology, or deliberate suppression of evidence.

None of the sources above establishes those claims. A dramatic title is not a source. Humanity really does keep needing that reminder.

## Human review instructions

2. Read the cited abstract, section, table, page, or original papyrus translation, not just a search result.
3. Record the exact page, section, table, papyrus identifier, or catalogue number.
4. Mark each source **APPROVED** or **REJECTED** only after inspection. Leave all others **UNREVIEWED**.
5. Add claim records to `data/evidence_ledger.json` only when the supporting sources are approved and the exact statement has been checked.
6. Use **DEBATED** for interpretations or scholarly disagreement and **UNKNOWN** when the evidence does not establish an answer.
7. Keep `include_in_script: false` until the claim has passed the human evidence review and the production gate.
