## Editorial rules

- Search each episode by its own topic, not by the broad channel niche.
- Distinguish direct observations, scholarly interpretations, and unresolved questions.
- Do not invent or overstate claims to make a more dramatic hook.
- Keep OCR, transliteration, segmentation, and translation separate.
- Label modern-slang rewrites as creative adaptations, never literal translations.
- No source or claim is approved by discovery, ranking, this package, or automated tests.
- The Great Pyramid phrase “oldest cover-up” is unsupported by the listed evidence; do not present a cover-up as fact.

The production gate remains closed until every planned video has sufficient human-reviewed, topic-matched evidence and a sourced shock hook.
